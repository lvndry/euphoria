These files are fields that Serato DJ wrote into audio files: Serato
Markers2, Serato Markers_ and Serato BeatGrid, for MP3 (ID3 GEOB data),
M4A (freeform atom text) and FLAC (Vorbis comment text).

They come from the Mixxx project's test data, src/test/serato/data, at
commit 20f2c218ae1b762ac39fd76ff408c8428056f4f7:
https://github.com/mixxxdj/mixxx/tree/20f2c218ae1b762ac39fd76ff408c8428056f4f7/src/test/serato/data
