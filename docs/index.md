# Euphoria

Euphoria finds the key and tempo of every track in your music library, so you can mix harmonically and match tempos without guessing. It is free, open source and works fully offline: no account, no activation, and your music never leaves your computer.

It is an open alternative to paid harmonic mixing tools such as Mixed In Key.

## What it does today

- Detects the key of a track, shown in Camelot notation (8A) and as a musical key (A minor).
- Detects the tempo in beats per minute.
- Analyzes whole folders from the command line, as a table or as JSON.
- Reads MP3, M4A (AAC), FLAC, WAV and AIFF.

Energy ratings, writing results into your DJ software, harmonic matching and a desktop app are in development.
