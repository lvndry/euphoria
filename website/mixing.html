Mix in key.

Two tracks in compatible keys blend without clashing. Euphoria finds the key of every track, shows which ones mix, and lets you see and hear why.

The Camelot wheel.

DJs write keys as Camelot codes: a number from 1 to 12 and a letter, A for minor and B for major. Neighbours on the wheel mix. Euphoria shows every key both ways, 8A and A minor.

One step around the wheel is a perfect fifth. Two notes a fifth apart, played into an oscilloscope, draw a 3 to 2 curve. That curve is the Euphoria logo.

Every move, as a curve.

Each move from 8A, drawn as the curve its two home notes trace. The names are the ones the app uses.

Our own key model.

Euphoria detects keys with drone-v2, a neural network trained by the Euphoria project and built into the app. It runs on your computer in plain Rust, with no GPU and nothing to download.

15% more tracks with the exact key than KeyFinder, the free key detector that Mixxx also builds in.
