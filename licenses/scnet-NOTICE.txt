SCNet XL attribution

Euphoria's Advanced mode graph export is adapted from the SCNet network,
https://github.com/starrytong/SCNet, as it is implemented in
Music-Source-Separation-Training,
https://github.com/ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training.
The network is described in "SCNet: Sparse Compression Network for Music
Source Separation" (Tong et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13276).
Both codes are licensed under the MIT License reproduced below.

The separately downloaded pretrained weights are the SCNet XL IHF checkpoint
model_scnet_ep_36_sdr_10.0891.ckpt, published in the v1.0.15 release of
Music-Source-Separation-Training:
https://github.com/ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training/releases/tag/v1.0.15
Euphoria does not redistribute them. The code license does not establish a
license for those weights. Their publisher states none, and the SCNet authors
say their own checkpoint was trained on the MUSDB dataset, whose audio is
licensed for research use.
